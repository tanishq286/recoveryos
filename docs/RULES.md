# RecoveryOS - Hard Rules

1. Rs.0 budget. Free tiers only. No paid API or service without explicit founder approval.
2. Fresh repo per project. Never read, touch, or reference other repositories/projects.
3. No mention of the building assistant or its platform anywhere in product, repo, copy, commits, or metadata.
4. Honesty constraints (from blueprint):
   - Results are lead / possible match / independently verified / not found - never fabricated
   - No guaranteed recovery, amount, timeline, or insurance cover
   - Heir/deceased-holder cases always route to human specialist review
   - Milestones move only with evidence (reference numbers, receipts, dated proof)
   - Government self-service links shown beside paid assistance
   - IEPF-5 has no government filing fee; never imply otherwise
5. Never ask users for OTPs, passwords, demat/banking logins, or government-portal credentials - in product copy, forms, or communication.
6. Never take possession of client assets. Money moves authority -> claimant directly.
7. Insurance is a planned benefit until a licensed partner and issuance path exist. All public wording stays conditional.
8. Triage rules are deterministic and versioned (RULES_VERSION in assets/app.js). No LLM invents eligibility criteria.
9. Commits: small, structured, one concern each, imperative subject.
10. Preview -> QA -> production. Nothing ships unviewed.
