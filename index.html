01 - The problem

Families lose track. Paperwork keeps the score.

India has decades of investments sitting unclaimed: shares bought in the physical-certificate era, dividends that never reached the right bank account, mutual fund folios from a forgotten SIP, provident fund balances left behind at a first job.

They are not gone. After seven years unclaimed, shares and dividends move to the government's Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) - from where rightful owners and heirs can still claim them. Mutual funds, PF and bank deposits have their own official routes.

The hard part was never eligibility. It is the process: which form, which authority, which proof, which office, and what happens after you file. RecoveryOS exists to make that path visible - and walk it with you.

05 - Pricing

You pay when money comes back. Not before.

No advance fees, no “file charges”, no percentage of a hope. The official IEPF-5 claim form itself carries no government filing fee - our fee is for the professional work, and it is contingent on recovery.

₹0
Until recovery

The free check, your evidence room, document preparation, filing and every follow-up along the way. If nothing is recovered, you owe nothing.

Third-party expenses (notarization, postage) are always disclosed before they are incurred.

10%
Success commission

Our only fee: 10% of the recovered amount, charged when your assets are actually credited back to you. The fee basis is written into your signed agreement before work begins.

Taxes apply as per law and are shown on your invoice. Shares and non-cash recoveries use a defined valuation basis in the agreement.

+10%
Insurance allocation

A separate amount, equal to 10% of your recovery, is set aside toward a life or health insurance policy for you - so a recovery can leave your family protected, not just repaid.

A planned benefit, not a promise of cover. It becomes a real policy only if a licensed insurer issues one for an eligible, consenting client. If no suitable policy is issued, or it costs less, the unused amount is returned to you.

Worked example - on a recovery of ₹1,00,000: our commission is ₹10,000; up to ₹10,000 goes toward your insurance cover through a licensed partner; ₹80,000 stays with you, before any disclosed taxes or third-party expenses. You approve every line in writing first.
06 - The honest fine print

You can file everything yourself. Here is where.

Every route we work is a public, official process. If your case is simple, the free check may be all you need - these links take you straight to the source. We exist for the cases that are not simple.

Shares & dividends (IEPF)

Unclaimed for 7+ years, assets move to the Investor Education and Protection Fund. Claim with Form IEPF-5 - no government filing fee.

iepf.gov.in - claimant guideMinistry of Corporate Affairs

Mutual funds (MITRA)

SEBI's MITRA platform traces inactive and unclaimed mutual fund folios; redemption follows the AMC/RTA path.

sebi.gov.in - MITRA circularSecurities and Exchange Board of India

Provident fund (EPFO)

EPF balances stay with the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation. Claims run through the member portal with your UAN.

epfindia.gov.in - unified portalEmployees' Provident Fund Organisation

Bank deposits (UDGAM)

RBI's UDGAM portal lets you search unclaimed deposits across multiple banks in one place.

udgam.rbi.org.inReserve Bank of India

Unclaimed MF amounts (AMFI)

The Association of Mutual Funds in India publishes unclaimed-amount resources and the standard operating process.

amfiindia.com - unclaimed amountsAssociation of Mutual Funds in India
07 - Trust, in writing

Rules we will never break.

Recovery scams are real, and they usually start with a phone call asking for an OTP. Here is exactly how RecoveryOS works - so you can hold us to it.

We never ask for OTPs, passwords, demat or banking logins, or government-portal credentials. Anyone who does is not us.

Money never touches us

No guaranteed outcomes

We never promise a recovery amount, a discovery, or a timeline. Authorities decide; we prepare, file, follow up, and show you the proof of each step.

Evidence or it did not happen

Your data stays yours

Humans where it matters

Identity, heirship and legal judgment are reviewed by people, and official submissions are made by people. Software assists; it never signs.
